Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 17, 2026

Night Meditations is designed to provide Scripture-based audio meditations without requiring users to create an account or provide personal information.

Information We Collect

Night Meditations does not collect, store, sell, or share personal information from users.

The app does not require an account or sign-in and does not use advertising or third-party tracking.

Information Stored on Your Device

Night Meditations may store information locally on your device, such as playback state, sleep timer settings, downloaded content, and other preferences necessary for the app to function. This information remains on your device and is not collected by us.

Third-Party Services

Night Meditations does not use third-party advertising or tracking services to collect personal information.

Children's Privacy

Night Meditations does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

If Night Meditations changes in a way that affects how user information is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.
